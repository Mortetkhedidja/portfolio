03 — The Problem

Volunteering needs more than disconnected communication.

Organizing volunteering activities involves several connected tasks: publishing initiatives, receiving registrations, coordinating participants and keeping track of their involvement.

When these activities are managed through separate channels, information can become difficult to organize and follow. عُضد was developed to bring these activities into one digital environment.

04 — Requirements

What the platform needs to support.

  • Provide an organized environment for volunteering campaigns.
  • Maintain volunteer profiles and participation information.
  • Support committee and administrator workflows.
  • Associate digital volunteer cards with verified participation.
  • Allow access to CV information according to volunteer consent.
  • Provide a responsive interface suitable for different devices.

05 — My Role

From platform structure to implementation.

I worked on the design and development of the platform, including its interface, application workflows and Firebase integration. The work involved organizing the different user-facing areas and connecting them to the application's data and authentication services.

I also worked on the digital card workflow, consent-related CV access and email notification integration.

06 — Technical Challenges

Connecting different roles and workflows.

  1. Role separation: organizing access for administrators, committees and volunteers.
  2. Participation verification: ensuring that digital card issuance is associated with the required verified participation.
  3. Data access: handling volunteer information and CV visibility according to consent.
  4. Email integration: connecting application events to an external email delivery service.
  5. Responsive experience: making the platform usable across desktop and mobile screens.

08 — Implementation Approach

Building the platform in connected modules.

  1. Organizing the application pages and user-facing workflows.
  2. Connecting authentication and database services.
  3. Implementing campaign and volunteer management interfaces.
  4. Developing the participation-based digital card workflow.
  5. Integrating email notifications and consent-related document access.
  6. Deploying the platform for online access and testing.

09 — Results

An accessible online volunteering platform.

The project resulted in a deployed web platform that brings volunteering campaigns, volunteer information, participation records and digital card functionality into one environment.

The live deployment and source code are available through the project links above.

10 — Lessons Learned

What this project taught me.

  • Multi-role platforms require clear access rules and carefully organized workflows.
  • Personal information should be shared according to explicit consent.
  • External services need reliable integration boundaries.
  • Deploying early makes it easier to test the experience beyond the development environment.
